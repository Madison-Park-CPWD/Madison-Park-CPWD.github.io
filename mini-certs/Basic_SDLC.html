Software Development Concepts Certificate

Prerequisites: None

Final Goal: Students will understand why software projects are organized into a life cycle, compare the major methodologies used to run that life cycle, and understand how a project's major roles fit into its phases and what each one actually produces.

Learning Targets

  • Define the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and explain why methodology choice is an organizational decision, not a matter of one approach being inherently best
  • Compare the Agile and Waterfall approaches to running that life cycle
  • Identify the major roles on a software project team, which SDLC phase(s) each works in most, and the main artifacts each one produces

Waterfall Methodology DESE Standard 4

Success Indicators

Students are ready to build on SDLC fundamentals when they can:

  • Explain, for an unfamiliar project scenario, which methodology fits best and why
  • Look at an artifact (a vision statement, an ER diagram, a test case) and correctly identify which role produced it and which phase it belongs to
  • Explain what goes wrong on a project when a phase gets skipped or a role's artifact never gets produced
